About Me

I'm Mohammed Nihal, a self-taught front-end developer and graphic designer from Kasaragod, Kerala — currently pursuing Computer Science and Engineering. I began by designing websites and logos for small startups on a tight budget, driven by a passion to support local ideas with great design. Now, I'm exploring everything from UI development to native tools, always learning and building with curiosity. I care deeply about thoughtful, secure, and human-centered software.



My Vision

To master programming at a fundamental level and build secure, intelligent systems that solve real-world problems — with clarity, care, and creativity.


My Mission

To continuously learn, design, and develop tools that empower people — combining thoughtful interfaces with powerful, reliable code that makes technology feel intuitive and meaningful.
